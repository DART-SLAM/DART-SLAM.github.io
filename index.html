Method

A physical reference when images are no longer enough.

DART-SLAM verifies live observations against known twin geometry, then uses accepted constraints to correct pose and scale in the physical frame.

01

Track

Monocular SLAM builds the local camera state and Gaussian map.

02

Verify

Image evidence is checked against a twin view with known pose and geometry.

03

Constrain

A verified match restores metric pose and scale; the constraint can recur when needed.
